The essentials

Battery details where
you already look.

Batlet lives in the menu bar. Open it when you need the full picture, and let alerts bring important changes to you.

01

All your devices, together

See your Mac, trusted iPhone or iPad, AirPods, and supported Bluetooth accessories in one compact panel.

02

Alerts with purpose

Know when a battery runs low, a device finishes charging, or a connection changes.

03

Make the view yours

Pin, hide, and rename devices. Choose how battery levels and alerts appear.

Open by design

Take a look inside.

Batlet is built in Swift, published under GPLv3, and available to build from source. You can inspect it, improve it, and share your changes under the same license.

Build Batlet on your Mac ↗
A few things to know

Bluetooth access is needed for supported accessories. iPhone and iPad battery levels require a device that has already trusted your Mac. Detection depends on what each device exposes to macOS.
